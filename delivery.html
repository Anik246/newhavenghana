Milestone-led delivery

Each phase earns the next.

PHASE 01

Land & community

Confirm ownership, boundaries, mining interests, existing obligations and community priorities.

Decision milestone

Verified site dossier and a clear route to land control.

PHASE 02

Technical feasibility

Survey pits and topography; assess contamination, groundwater, flooding, ground stability and infrastructure access.

Decision milestone

A technically supported land-use suitability plan.

PHASE 03

Reclamation design

Develop earthworks, remediation, drainage, revegetation and monitoring plans. Establish costs and approval requirements.

Decision milestone

An agreed, costed reclamation and verification strategy.

PHASE 04

Master planning

Prepare alternative layouts, infrastructure designs, market assessment and development feasibility.

Decision milestone

A feasible proposal supported by stakeholder engagement and required approvals.

PHASE 05

Phased delivery

Reclaim and verify suitable areas, install infrastructure and deliver development in manageable stages.

Decision milestone

Independent verification and funding before each construction phase.
